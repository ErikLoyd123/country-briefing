## Vietnam

### Transparency and corruption in practice

Ranked 81st, improving one point a year [@VN-15]. The campaign against corruption is real and it has a side effect: officials delay decisions and spending to avoid exposure; in one Ho Chi Minh City period about 100 public projects got no planned funding [@VN-66]. At the January 2026 Party Congress, To Lam said all wrongdoing must be dealt with, while setting a growth target of at least 10 percent a year [@VN-75].

### Trade partners and agreements

18 free trade agreements [@VN-63], including CPTPP, the EU deal, RCEP and a new UK agreement [@VN-64]. The US is the biggest export market and the source of the tariff [@VN-45; @VN-43]; China supplies 40 percent of imports [@VN-46]. Xi Jinping visited in April 2025 and both sides pledged calm at sea [@VN-72]. To Lam visited Putin on 7 to 9 September 2026, then flew to France [@VN-88].

### Civil unrest

Rare and specific. In May 2014 at least 20 people died in riots at foreign-owned factories after China moved an oil rig into disputed waters [@VN-67]. In June 2018 protests against 99-year land leases and the cybersecurity law led to about 200 arrests [@VN-68]. Both were triggered by China and by land. Watch those two.

### Industries most exposed to politics

- **Electronics:** 23 percent of exports, US$42 billion of it to the US; Samsung alone is 12 percent of national exports [@VN-70; @VN-69].
- **Footwear and apparel:** US$29 billion of footwear exports, US$11 billion to the US, now under the 12.5 percent Section 301 tariff [@CR-22; @VN-96].
- **Platforms and payments:** cybersecurity, data and payments decrees rewrite the rules every year [@VN-28; @MC-19].
- **Energy:** the revised power plan targets 28 to 36 percent renewables by 2030 after the 2023 blackouts [@VN-71; @VN-50].

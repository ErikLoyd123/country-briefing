## Singapore

### Crocs: the regional base

Crocs Asia Pte. Ltd. has been incorporated in Singapore since December 2004, at The Metropolis in Buona Vista [@CR-19], in an 18,000 square foot office finished in 2018 [@CR-20]. It runs 18 company stores here [@CR-17] and uses a third-party distribution centre [@CR-18].

**The risk it hit.** A 10 percent US tariff on Singapore-origin goods that the free trade agreement did not stop [@SG-46; @SG-47]. Minor for Crocs, whose Singapore business is retail and logistics, not manufacturing [@CR-24].

**What it did.** Kept Singapore as the regional base: predictable rules, no capital controls [@SG-60], and the region's best logistics [@SG-12].

### Mastercard: Asia Pacific headquarters

Singapore is Mastercard's Asia Pacific headquarters [@MC-1], home to one of its six global Experience Centres [@MC-2; @MC-3] and, from later in 2026, a regional AI Centre of Excellence [@MC-4].

**The risk around it.** A tough, transparent regulator. The Payment Services Act licenses seven payment activities [@MC-7; @MC-8], and MAS fined nine financial institutions S$27 million in July 2025 over the S$3 billion 2023 laundering case [@MC-10; @MC-11]. Sanctions reach card networks too: Mastercard suspended Russia in 2022 [@MC-26].

**What it did.** Used the predictability. First live AI-agent payment with DBS and UOB in March 2026 [@MC-6], then agentic transactions across ASEAN starting from Singapore [@MC-5]. Cross-border links like PayNow to India's UPI show the regulator will connect systems when the rules are met [@MC-9].

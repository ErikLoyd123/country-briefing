The perceived risks of doing business, in the four areas the assignment names: government, society, security, economy. Ranked by how much they should change what you do. Readings come from Coface, the World Bank Logistics Performance Index, Transparency International and the rating agencies. (The CIA World Factbook was discontinued in February 2026, so we substituted government sources.)

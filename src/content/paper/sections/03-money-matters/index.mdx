What all of that means for foreign direct investment: the rules, the caps, the incentives, and whether you can get your money out.

## At a glance

|  | Singapore | Vietnam |
|---|---|---|
| FDI | S$3,130 billion of FDI stock (end 2024), up 9.5 percent [@SG-54] | US$38.4 billion registered in 2025; US$27.6 billion disbursed, a five-year high [@VN-55]. January to August 2026: US$40.6 billion registered, up 55 percent [@VN-56] |
| Corporate tax | 17 percent headline [@SG-59] | 20 percent standard [@VN-58] |
| Getting money out | No capital controls [@SG-60] and no dividend withholding [@SG-61] | Profits remitted once a year, after audited accounts and tax finalisation [@VN-60] |
| Land | Landed residential property: sales to foreigners without approval are void [@SG-57] | Use rights only, no ownership [@VN-61] |

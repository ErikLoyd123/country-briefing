## Singapore

### Where the money is welcome

- The US is the largest single investor, with US$424 billion of FDI stock [@SG-55]. EDB landed S$14.2 billion of fixed-asset commitments in 2025 [@SG-65].
- No blanket cap on foreign ownership. Incentives can cut tax to 5 or 10 percent [@SG-56; @SG-58].
- Setting up costs S$315 in government fees [@SG-64]. The US-Singapore FTA has been in force since 2004 [@SG-63].

### Where it is not

- Broadcasting: no foreign source may hold 49 percent or more [@SG-56].
- Landed residential property: sales to foreigners without approval are void [@SG-57].
- Tax planning: the 15 percent global minimum tax applies from 2025, so the incentive game is smaller than it was [@SG-62].

<Callout title="Getting money out" icon="briefcase" size="sm">
Profits and capital move freely, with no withholding on dividends [@SG-60; @SG-61]. This is the single biggest practical difference from Vietnam.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Seen at Mastercard" size="sm">
Mastercard ran its first live AI-agent payment in Singapore in March 2026 with DBS and UOB, and launched agentic transactions across ASEAN from here a month later [@MC-6; @MC-5]. Singapore is where a regulated payments company tests new things first, because the regulator is predictable [@MC-7].
</Callout>

- **ASEAN:** Association of Southeast Asian Nations, the ten-country regional bloc both countries belong to.
- **CPI:** Corruption Perceptions Index, Transparency International's annual 0 to 100 score; higher is cleaner. (Also consumer price index, the inflation measure, where the context is prices.)
- **CPTPP:** Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership, a 12-economy trade pact; both countries are members.
- **EDB:** Singapore Economic Development Board, the agency that courts and approves foreign investment.
- **EVFTA:** EU-Vietnam Free Trade Agreement, in force since August 2020.
- **FDI:** Foreign direct investment: money a company puts into operations in another country, as opposed to buying its shares.
- **FICA:** Foreign Interference (Countermeasures) Act, Singapore's 2021 law against foreign influence in domestic politics.
- **FTSE Russell:** An index provider; its reclassification of Vietnam from frontier to emerging market pulls in index-tracking funds.
- **GST:** Goods and Services Tax, Singapore's 9 percent sales tax.
- **LPI:** World Bank Logistics Performance Index, a 1 to 5 score of how easily goods move through a country.
- **MAS:** Monetary Authority of Singapore, the central bank and financial regulator.
- **MTI:** Singapore Ministry of Trade and Industry, which publishes the official growth forecast.
- **NAPAS:** National Payment Corporation of Vietnam, the state-owned domestic card switch.
- **PAP:** People's Action Party, in government in Singapore since independence.
- **POFMA:** Protection from Online Falsehoods and Manipulation Act, Singapore's 2019 law allowing correction orders on online statements.
- **RCEP:** Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership, a 15-country Asia-Pacific trade agreement including China; both countries are members.
- **Reciprocal tariff:** The 2025 US import duties set country by country: 10 percent on Singapore, 20 percent on Vietnam.
- **Transshipment:** Routing goods through a third country to disguise their origin; the US charges 40 percent on goods it judges transshipped through Vietnam.
- **USSFTA:** United States-Singapore Free Trade Agreement, in force since 2004.

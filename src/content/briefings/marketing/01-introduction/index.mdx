<Slide title="Singapore and Vietnam, side by side" lede="The short answer to each question; the sections that follow give the evidence.">

<Fragment slot="sg">
1. **Values:** group-minded and hierarchical, but deal-hungry (kiasu) and persuaded by proof.
2. **Demographics:** 6.1 million people, older (median 43.7) and highly educated: promotions must inform.
3. **Buying habits:** rich but squeezed by the cost of living; more than half hunt discounts.
4. **Channels:** YouTube, LinkedIn and Meta for reach; Shopee for the sale. Social selling is still small.
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
1. **Values:** family, word of mouth and a trusted country of origin drive choices.
2. **Demographics:** 102 million people, young (median 33.4) and mostly rural: promotions must be visual and simple.
3. **Buying habits:** price comes first, but quality and safety matter; cash on delivery still rules online.
4. **Channels:** Facebook, Zalo and TikTok; customers message brands and buy on livestreams.
</Fragment>

<Slide title="Why they shop the way they do" lede="Singapore's caution comes from the cost of living. Vietnam's comes from income, but quality concerns are rising." sgMedia="singapore/hawker-centre.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/hanoi-market.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Cost of living** is the top worry for 66%, and 63% say inflation has cut their purchasing power.
- **Careful, not stopped:** 52% are cautious but still buy most of what they need.
- **What it rewards:** value bundles, member pricing and visible discounts on a premium base.
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Price first:** 47% name price as the most important factor when choosing food.
- **But quality matters:** 74% worry about pesticide residues and ultra-processed foods.
- **Convenience is growing:** 54% buy prepared food at least once a week.
- **What it rewards:** small, affordable pack sizes and visible proof of safety.
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="How and when they pay" lede="Singapore mixes cash, QR codes and cards. Vietnam has leapt to e-wallets, but cash on delivery still rules online." sgMedia="video/contactless-payment.mp4" vnMedia="vietnam/smartphone-chat.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Payment methods used:** cash 63%, scan-to-pay 56%, credit cards 52%.
- **When:** discounts cluster around festive seasons and the year-end online sales.
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Payment methods used:** e-wallets 62%, cash 49%, QR codes 37%, credit cards 37%.
- **Online:** cash on delivery is still the most preferred way to pay; 49% have tried buy-now-pay-later.
- **When:** 85% shop around Tết, and the 10.10, 11.11 and 12.12 sales stretch the peak season across the last quarter.
</Fragment>

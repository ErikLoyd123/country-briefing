<Slide title="What moves a purchase" lede="Singaporeans discover on social media but buy on marketplaces. Vietnamese talk to brands directly and buy while they watch." sgMedia="video/sg-street-crossing.mp4" vnMedia="vietnam/smartphone-chat.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Social selling is small:** only 16% bought on TikTok Shop, Facebook Marketplace or Instagram Shop in six months [@SG-13].
- **Creators drive discovery:** 58% of TikTok shoppers found new products through content creators [@SG-14].
- **Trust is earned:** about a third have no trust at all in influencer-endorsed products [@SG-15], so reviews and credible experts matter more than celebrity.
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Messaging is the storefront:** 82.8% prefer messaging to talk to a business, and 61.2% message one every day [@VN-16].
- **Livestreams sell:** 35% have joined a livestream shopping event [@VN-14].
- **Facebook at peak season:** 63% turned to Facebook and 58% to Messenger while holiday shopping [@VN-17].
- **Mobile only:** 92% of online shoppers buy on a smartphone [@VN-14].
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="Privacy rules for marketers" lede="Both countries require consent before using personal data. Vietnam's new law, in force since January 2026, carries Singapore-sized fines." sgMedia="singapore/raffles-place.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/landmark-81.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA):** consent and a clear purpose before collecting or using data.
- **Do Not Call Registry:** marketing calls and texts must be checked against it; fines up to S$1 million for an organisation [@SG-17].
- **Data breaches:** fines up to 10% of Singapore turnover (above S$10 million), or S$1 million, since October 2022 [@SG-16].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Personal Data Protection Law:** passed June 2025, in force 1 January 2026; consent remains the basis [@VN-20].
- **Cross-border transfers:** fines up to 5% of the previous year's revenue, which matters for regional CRM and ad platforms [@VN-21].
- **Selling data:** fines up to ten times the revenue earned; most other breaches are capped at 3bn ₫ (about US$115,000) [@VN-21].
- **Small firms** get five years' grace on some paperwork [@VN-22].
</Fragment>

</Slide>

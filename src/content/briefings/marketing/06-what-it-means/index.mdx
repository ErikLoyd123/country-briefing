<Takeaway media="singapore/orchard-street.jpg" label="Singapore">
A small, rich, sceptical market: prove it, price it smartly, and respect the PDPA.

<Fragment slot="detail">Lead with evidence and value bundles, localize for four communities' festivals, and use YouTube and LinkedIn for reach and marketplaces for the sale. Check every marketing list against the Do Not Call Registry.</Fragment>
</Takeaway>
<Takeaway media="vietnam/saigon-skyline.jpg" label="Vietnam">
A young, fast-growing market: be trusted, be on the phone, be affordable.

<Fragment slot="detail">Sell through Facebook, Zalo and TikTok with livestreams and direct messaging, offer cash on delivery and small pack sizes, and build consent records for the new data law from day one.</Fragment>
</Takeaway>

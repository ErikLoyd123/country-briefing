<Slide title="What that means at the till" lede="Group values show up differently: Singapore's shoppers chase value and certainty; Vietnam's follow family, friends and trusted origins." sgMedia="singapore/hawker-centre.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/coffee-meeting.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Many cultures, one market:** citizens are 75.5% Chinese, 15.1% Malay and 7.6% Indian [@SG-2]. Campaigns follow Chinese New Year, Hari Raya and Deepavali, and halal certification matters for food.
- **Kiasu ("fear of losing out"):** shoppers compare, hunt deals and join loyalty schemes. Limited-time offers and clear guarantees work.
- **Respect for authority:** official endorsements, awards and expert proof carry weight; exaggerated claims backfire.
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Family decides:** big purchases are group decisions, and word of mouth from friends and family is the most trusted channel.
- **Face and gifting:** Tết and other festivals drive gift buying, where brand and packaging signal respect.
- **Origin is a quality signal:** nearly 60% hold a very positive view of Japanese products and 42% of American ones, but only 15% of Chinese ones [@VN-2]. Younger buyers trust Vietnamese brands more than their parents do [@VN-3].
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="What it means for a marketer" layout="joined">

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
<Takeaway media="singapore/little-india.jpg" label="Singapore">
Prove the value, then localize for each community.

<Fragment slot="detail">Lead with evidence, guarantees and deals; plan the calendar around four communities' festivals rather than one.</Fragment>
</Takeaway>
<Takeaway media="vietnam/coffee-meeting.jpg" label="Vietnam">
Earn the family's trust, and say where it's from.

<Fragment slot="detail">Referrals, community and a credible country of origin (Japan, the US) do more than price cuts alone.</Fragment>
</Takeaway>
</div>

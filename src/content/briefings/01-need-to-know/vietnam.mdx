## Vietnam

<HintCards title="Ten things to know on arrival">

1. **Get the e-visa first.** Apply online before you go: US$25 single entry, US$50 multiple, valid up to 90 days [@VN-1].
2. **Mind your phone.** The US advisory is Level 1, but petty theft in tourist areas is common. Hold your phone away from the street [@VN-2].
3. **Traffic is the first culture shock.** There are 77 million motorbikes for 100 million people [@VN-3].
4. **Cross at a steady pace.** Step out, walk steadily, never stop or dart back. The bikes flow around you [@VN-4].
5. **Carry cash.** Cash is still king in small places; cards work in hotels and larger venues. Tipping is not expected [@VN-5].
6. **Declare large cash.** Declare cash above 15 million dong or US$5,000 at the border [@VN-8].
7. **Insure for evacuation.** Local hospitals do not take US insurance. Carry travel insurance with medical evacuation [@VN-9].
8. **Do not rent a motorbike.** Without a Vietnamese licence it is illegal and voids your insurance [@VN-10].
9. **Drug tests look back.** Enforcement uses blood tests. You can be charged for something you took before you arrived [@VN-42].
10. **Tet shuts the country.** In 2026 it ran 14 to 22 February; plan nothing then [@VN-6].

</HintCards>

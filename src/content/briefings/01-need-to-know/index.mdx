<Slide title="Ten things to know on arrival" sgMedia="singapore/jewel-changi.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/motorbikes-at-lights.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">

1. **No visa for short visits.** 90 days for US citizens; six months left on your passport [@SG-2].
2. **File the arrival card.** Free, online, within three days of landing [@SG-1].
3. **English works everywhere.** Business, government and daily life [@SG-3].
4. **Bring business cards.** The first thing exchanged, with both hands [@SG-4].
5. **Read the "++" on menus.** Service charge and GST are added; do not tip on top [@SG-6].

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">

1. **Get the e-visa first.** US$25 single entry, US$50 multiple, up to 90 days [@VN-1].
2. **Mind your phone.** Level 1 advisory, but petty theft is common in tourist areas [@VN-2].
3. **Expect the traffic.** 77 million motorbikes for about 100 million people [@VN-3].
4. **Cross at a steady pace.** Never stop or dart back; the bikes flow around you [@VN-4].
5. **Carry cash.** Cash is king in small places; no tipping expected [@VN-5].

</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="And five more" sgMedia="singapore/hawker-centre.jpg" vnMedia="video/rainy-day-traffic.mp4">

<Fragment slot="sg">

6. **Leave the vape at home.** Fines up to S$10,000 since May 2026 [@SG-9].
7. **No chewing gum.** Importing it is prohibited outright [@SG-10].
8. **Crime is rare.** Crime against foreigners is rare [@SG-8].
9. **Expect rain.** About 171 rain days a year [@SG-11].
10. **Be on time.** First in the world on logistics; things arrive on time, and so should you [@SG-12].

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">

6. **Declare large cash.** Over 15 million dong or US$5,000 at the border [@VN-8].
7. **Insure for evacuation.** Local hospitals do not take US insurance [@VN-9].
8. **Do not rent a motorbike.** Illegal without a Vietnamese licence; voids insurance [@VN-10].
9. **Drug tests look back.** You can be charged for something taken before you arrived [@VN-42].
10. **Tet shuts the country.** 14 to 22 February in 2026; plan nothing then [@VN-6].

</Fragment>

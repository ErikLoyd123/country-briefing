<Slide title="Five risks to know on arrival" covers={['Security']} lede="The ones that can get a visitor arrested, robbed or hurt." sgMedia="singapore/supreme-court-night.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/motorbikes-at-lights.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">

1. **Drug laws carry the death penalty.** Trafficking can mean execution; possession can mean caning [@SG-41; @SG-81].
2. **Petty offences are arrestable.** Jaywalking, littering or spitting can lead to arrest, and vandalism means caning [@SG-42].
3. **Leave the vape at home.** Possessing or using one can cost S$10,000 since May 2026 [@SG-9].
4. **Expect scams, not street crime.** 37,308 scam cases and S$913 million lost in 2025 [@SG-38; @SG-39].
5. **The terror threat is rated high.** Driven by online self-radicalisation [@SG-92].

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">

1. **The road is the biggest danger.** 8,515 traffic deaths in the first ten months of 2025 [@VN-35].
2. **Do not rent a motorbike.** Illegal without a Vietnamese licence, and it voids your insurance [@VN-10].
3. **Hold on to your phone and bag.** Drive-by snatching is common; use established taxi firms [@VN-2; @VN-41].
4. **Drug tests look back.** You can be charged for something taken before you arrived [@VN-42].
5. **Insure for evacuation.** Local hospitals do not take US insurance [@VN-9].

</Fragment>

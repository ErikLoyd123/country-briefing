## Singapore

<HintCards title="Ten things to know on arrival">

1. **No visa for short visits.** US citizens need no visa for visits of 90 days or less; keep six months on your passport [@SG-2].
2. **File the arrival card.** File the free SG Arrival Card online within three days before you fly [@SG-1].
3. **English works everywhere.** English is the language of business, government and daily life. You will not need a phrasebook [@SG-3].
4. **Bring business cards.** They are the first thing exchanged, offered with both hands [@SG-4].
5. **Read the “++” on menus.** A 10 percent service charge plus 9 percent GST are added. Do not tip on top [@SG-6].
6. **Leave the vape at home.** Possession or use can cost S$10,000 since May 2026 [@SG-9].
7. **No chewing gum.** Importing it is prohibited outright [@SG-10].
8. **Crime is rare.** The US advisory is Level 1, "normal precautions," and crime against foreigners is rare [@SG-8].
9. **Expect rain.** About 171 rain days a year. Carry an umbrella to every meeting [@SG-11].
10. **Be on time.** You are landing in the world's best-ranked logistics hub, first on the World Bank index. Things arrive on time; so should you [@SG-12].

</HintCards>

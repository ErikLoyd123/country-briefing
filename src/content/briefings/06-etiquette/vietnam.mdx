## Vietnam

<HintCards title="In the room and on the street">

- **Business cards.** Cards with both hands, to the most senior person first; take time to read what you receive [@VN-76].
- **Rank and punctuality.** Enter and sit by rank, highest first. Be punctual and apologise if late [@VN-79].
- **Face.** Never disagree in public. Raise problems privately; loss of face ends relationships [@VN-77].
- **Small talk.** Start with small talk about family; avoid politics and the war. Decisions come slowly, by consensus [@VN-80].
- **Dress.** Dress modestly: suits for men, skirts and blouses for women, no bright colours [@VN-78].
- **Gifts.** Gifts with both hands, palms up. No clocks, knives, scissors, or black or white wrapping [@VN-81].
- **Small notes.** Carry dong in small notes; tipping is not expected [@VN-5].
- **Crossing the street.** Cross the street at a steady pace and let the bikes flow around you [@VN-4].
- **Taxis.** Use established taxi firms (Mai Linh, Vinasun) and keep bags away from the roadside [@VN-41].
- **Temper.** Never lose your temper. It costs you the relationship, not just the meeting [@VN-79].
- **Cash at the border.** Declare cash above US$5,000 at the border [@VN-8].

</HintCards>

<Callout title="Don't" tone="warn" size="sm">
Do not rent a motorbike (illegal without a Vietnamese licence) [@VN-10] and do not assume a drug taken elsewhere is safe here; blood tests are grounds for arrest [@VN-42].
</Callout>

<Slide title="In the room" lede="A few habits travel between the two. The rest do not." layout="joined">

<Trio>
<Fragment slot="sg">

- **Get to the point.** Discussion is direct; hours are 8:30 to 5:00 [@SG-79].
- **Surnames first.** Use last names until invited; if unsure, ask how to address them [@SG-78].
- **Long sleeves, no tie.** A jacket and tie only for formal occasions [@SG-77].

</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="both">

- **Business cards with both hands.** Read the card you receive before you put it away [@SG-4; @VN-76].
- **Stay off sensitive ground.** Race and religion are protected by law in Singapore; avoid politics and the war in Vietnam [@SG-80; @VN-80].

</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="vn">

- **Rank decides the order.** Cards to the most senior first; enter by rank; be punctual [@VN-76; @VN-79].
- **Never disagree in public.** Raise problems privately; lost face ends relationships [@VN-77].
- **Small talk first.** Family before business; decisions come slowly, by consensus [@VN-80].
- **Gifts with both hands.** No clocks, knives, scissors, or black or white wrapping [@VN-81].

</Fragment>
</Trio>

</Slide>

<Slide title="On the street" layout="joined">

<Trio>
<Fragment slot="sg">

- **Street rules are enforced.** Jaywalking, littering or spitting can lead to arrest [@SG-81].
- **No vapes, even for visitors.** Fines, confiscation and possible deportation [@SG-82].
- **No chewing gum through customs** [@SG-10].
- **Carry an umbrella.** It rains on 171 days a year [@SG-11].

</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="both">

- **Do not tip.** Singapore adds a service charge and GST; in Vietnam tipping is not expected [@SG-6; @VN-5].
- **Drug laws apply to you.** Death penalty for trafficking in Singapore; in Vietnam a blood test can mean arrest [@SG-41; @VN-42].

</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="vn">

- **Carry small notes.** Dong comes in notes only [@VN-5].
- **Cross at a steady pace.** The bikes flow around you [@VN-4].
- **Use established taxis.** Mai Linh or Vinasun; keep bags away from the roadside [@VN-41].
- **Declare cash over US$5,000** at the border [@VN-8].

</Fragment>
</Trio>

</Slide>

<Slide title="The mistakes that cost the most" layout="backdrop" sgMedia="singapore/hawker-centre.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/hanoi-market.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
Treating drug laws as negotiable. Trafficking carries the death penalty and possession can mean caning [@SG-41; @SG-81].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
Renting a motorbike, which is illegal without a Vietnamese licence, and losing your temper, which costs you the relationship [@VN-10; @VN-79].
</Fragment>

</Slide>

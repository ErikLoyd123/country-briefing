<Slide title="In the room" lede="A few habits travel between the two. The rest do not." layout="joined">

<Trio sgMedia="singapore/raffles-place.jpg" bothMedia="common/business-card-two-hands.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/coffee-meeting.jpg">
<Fragment slot="sg">

- **Get to the point.** Discussion is direct; hours are 8:30 to 5:00 [@SG-79].
- **Surnames first.** Use last names until invited; if unsure, ask how to address them [@SG-78].
- **Long sleeves, no tie.** A jacket and tie only for formal occasions [@SG-77]. For women, polished, modest separates or dresses, paired with closed-toe shoes and a layer for heavy indoor AC.

</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="both">

- **Business cards with both hands.** Read the card you receive before you put it away [@SG-4; @VN-76].
- **Stay off sensitive ground.** Race and religion are protected by law in Singapore; avoid politics and the war in Vietnam [@SG-80; @VN-80].

</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="vn">

- **Rank decides the order.** Cards to the most senior first; enter by rank; be punctual [@VN-76; @VN-79].
- **Never disagree in public.** Raise problems privately; lost face ends relationships [@VN-77].
- **Small talk first.** Family before business; decisions come slowly, by consensus [@VN-80].
- **Gifts with both hands.** No clocks, knives, scissors, or black or white wrapping [@VN-81].

</Fragment>
</Trio>

</Slide>

<Slide title="On the street" covers={['Security']} tone="tint" lede="The rest is in Need to Know. These are the ones people get caught by." layout="joined">

<Trio tall sgMedia="video/sg-street-crossing.mp4" bothMedia="singapore/sgd-banknotes.jpg" vnMedia="video/vn-motorbikes-day.mp4">
<Fragment slot="sg">

- **Street rules are enforced.** Jaywalking, littering or spitting can lead to arrest [@SG-81].

</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="both">

- **Do not tip.** Singapore adds a service charge and GST; in Vietnam tipping is not expected [@SG-6; @VN-5].

</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="vn">

- **Use established taxis.** Mai Linh or Vinasun, and keep bags away from the roadside [@VN-41].

</Fragment>
</Trio>

</Slide>

<Fragment slot="sg">
Treating drug laws as negotiable. Trafficking carries the death penalty and possession can mean caning [@SG-41; @SG-81].

<Fragment slot="vn">
Losing your temper or challenging someone in the meeting, which costs them face and you the relationship [@VN-77].

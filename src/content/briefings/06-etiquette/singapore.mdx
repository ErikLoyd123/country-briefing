## Singapore

<HintCards title="In the room and on the street">

- **Business cards.** Cards first, both hands, read it before you put it away [@SG-4].
- **Dress.** Long-sleeved shirt, no tie for most meetings; suit and tie for formal occasions [@SG-77].
- **Names.** Use last names until invited otherwise; if unsure, ask "How shall I address you?" [@SG-78].
- **Hours and pace.** Business hours are 8:30 to 5:00 and discussion is direct. Get to the point [@SG-79].
- **No tipping.** Do not tip on top of the “++” service charge and tax [@SG-6].
- **Race and religion.** Race and religion are legally protected ground; keep jokes and opinions out of the room [@SG-80].
- **Singlish.** English is the working language, but the accent and vocabulary (Singlish) take a day to tune into [@SG-3].
- **Street rules.** No jaywalking, littering or spitting; each can lead to arrest [@SG-81].
- **Vapes.** No vaping, anywhere, including for visitors; confiscation, fines and possible deportation [@SG-82].
- **Chewing gum.** No chewing gum through customs [@SG-10].
- **Umbrella.** Carry an umbrella; it rains 171 days a year [@SG-11].

</HintCards>

<Callout title="Don't" tone="warn" size="sm">
Do not treat drug laws as negotiable. Trafficking carries the death penalty and possession can mean caning [@SG-41; @SG-81].
</Callout>

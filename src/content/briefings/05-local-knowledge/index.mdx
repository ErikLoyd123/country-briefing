<Slide title="Mastercard: licensed innovator, and partner to the state" sgMedia="singapore/jewel-changi.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/hcmc-metro.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
**Asia Pacific headquarters** [@MC-1]. In March 2026 an AI agent paid for a ride to Changi Airport, Mastercard's first live agentic payment, with DBS and UOB [@MC-6].

**The risk it hit:** a tough, transparent regulator that licenses seven payment services and fined nine institutions S$27 million in 2025 [@MC-8; @MC-10].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">

**The risk it hit:** payment rules that rewrite themselves (next slide).
</Fragment>

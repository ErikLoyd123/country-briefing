<Slide title="Where the money is going" lede="Singapore draws most of Southeast Asia's investment. Ho Chi Minh City's inflows have already beaten last year's total.">

<Fragment slot="sg">
<BigStat value="US$151bn" unit="foreign investment, 2025">
Up from US$136bn, about 62% of Southeast Asia's total [@SG-39].
</BigStat>

- **Much of it passes through:** headquarters and treasury capital, not only new plants [@SG-39].
- **New commitments:** about US$11.1bn in 2025, 85% manufacturing, creating 15,700 jobs, two-thirds paying over about US$3,900 a month [@SG-40].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
<BigStat value="US$10.1bn" unit="Ho Chi Minh City, January to August 2026">
Already above 2025's full-year US$8.4bn [@VN-47; @VN-46].
</BigStat>

- **Nationally,** money actually spent by foreign investors has risen every year, to US$27.6bn in 2025 [@VN-48].
- **Buying in:** US$2.7bn came through stakes in local firms in seven months [@VN-49], a common entry route.
- **Industrial parks** are already 21% past their 2026 target [@VN-50].
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="Where the opportunities are" lede="Singapore pays for AI and research. Ho Chi Minh City offers land, a port and cheap tax in its new financial centre." sgMedia="singapore/server-racks.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/industrial-welding.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Sectors:** AI chips and servers, biopharma, specialty chemicals, aircraft maintenance and tech headquarters [@SG-40].
- **Tailwind:** AI spending lifted growth to 6.1% in the first half of 2026 [@SG-11].
- **Budget 2026:** a 400% tax deduction on AI spending, about US$29bn for research to 2030, and the Global Trader Programme extended to 2031 [@SG-41].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Sectors:** data centres, biomedical technology and smart electronics [@VN-49].
- **Scale:** since the merger the city has 66 industrial zones, the Cai Mep–Thi Vai deep-water port and over a quarter of national industrial output [@VN-51].
- **Financial centre:** 10% corporate tax for 30 years [@VN-52], but it must show real deals by November [@VN-53]. Promising, unproven.
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="What could go wrong" tone="tint" lede="Singapore's risks are external and cost-driven. Ho Chi Minh City's are power, delivery and prices.">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **US tariff:** 12.5% on about a third of exports to the US [@SG-30; @SG-31]. Pharmaceuticals, semiconductors and aerospace are exempt for now [@SG-42], so the exemption list is the variable to watch.
- **AI cycle:** growth leans on one investment boom [@SG-11].
- **Costs:** core inflation fell from 4.2% in 2023 to 0.7% in 2025 [@SG-43; @SG-44] but is rising again, and 71.8% of firms name rising costs as their top concern [@SG-45].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Power:** many city substations already run at full capacity [@VN-54], and Vietnam expects a 4,200 MW shortfall in 2027 [@VN-55].
- **Delivery:** only a quarter of 2026's public investment budget was spent by late June [@VN-56]. Treat official targets as ceilings.
- **Prices and currency:** inflation is at the 4.5% target [@VN-57] and the dong is expected to weaken another 2.5 to 3% [@VN-58]. Write escalation clauses into contracts.
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="The rules for foreign investors" lede="Singapore is open but no longer low-tax. Vietnam is loosening, step by step.">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Open:** no exchange controls [@SG-6], and capital and profits move freely [@SG-7].
- **Minimum tax:** large multinationals pay at least 15% from January 2025 [@SG-41], so Singapore now competes with grants and credits rather than low rates.
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Simpler approvals:** the amended Investment Law took effect on 1 March 2026, though its transitional rules are still settling [@VN-59].
- **Capital controls** still apply outside the financial centre [@VN-52].
- **Rating:** BB+ with S&P and Fitch; Moody's outlook positive since May 2026 [@VN-21; @VN-22; @VN-23].
- **Markets:** FTSE Russell's emerging-market upgrade took effect on 21 September 2026 [@VN-60].
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="Before you commit" lede="Practical steps, in each country and across both." layout="joined">

<Trio sgMedia="singapore/marina-bay-financial.jpg" bothMedia="singapore/strait-shipping.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/binh-duong-industrial-park.jpg">
<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Talk to EDB early.** Incentives are awarded case by case.
- **Budget for a rising currency.** Build a firmer Singapore dollar into cost forecasts [@SG-3].
- **Check the tariff list.** Match each product against the US exemptions [@SG-42].
</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="both">
- **Hub and spoke.** Singapore for holding, treasury and IP; Ho Chi Minh City for production.
- **Already the pattern.** Singapore is the largest source of new investment in Vietnam, at 35.1% [@VN-61].
</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Confirm site power.** Check supply before signing a lease [@VN-54].
- **Match currencies.** Pair dollar revenue with dollar costs as the dong weakens [@VN-58].
- **Keep origin records.** Document every input for US tariff reviews [@VN-36].
</Fragment>
</Trio>

</Slide>

<Slide title="The verdict" layout="joined">

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
<Takeaway media="singapore/raffles-place.jpg" label="Singapore: GO">
For regional headquarters, treasury, IP, R&D and high-value manufacturing. Not for labour-intensive production.

<Fragment slot="detail">Plan for changes to the US tariff exemption list [@SG-42], reliance on the AI cycle [@SG-11] and high operating costs [@SG-45].</Fragment>
</Takeaway>
<Takeaway media="vietnam/saigon-river-containers.jpg" label="Ho Chi Minh City: CONDITIONAL GO">
For export manufacturing in the industrial belt and services in the city centre.

<Fragment slot="detail">Conditional on reliable power [@VN-55], delivery against targets [@VN-56] and managing currency and cost pressure [@VN-58].</Fragment>
</Takeaway>
</div>

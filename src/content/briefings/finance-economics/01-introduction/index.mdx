<Slide title="Five things to know before you commit" lede="The numbers that most change the cost, risk or timing of an investment." sgMedia="singapore/raffles-place.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/saigon-skyline.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">

1. **Stability, not speed.** Long-run growth of 2 to 3% a year [@SG-22].
2. **The dollar is set to rise.** Budget for increases in local payroll and leases [@SG-3].
3. **Money moves freely.** No exchange controls on capital or profits [@SG-6; @SG-7].
4. **No longer low-tax.** Large multinationals pay at least 15% [@SG-19].
5. **Watch the US tariff.** 12.5% on about a third of domestic exports to the US [@SG-31].

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">

1. **Fast, but short of target.** 8.55% growth against a 10.2% goal [@VN-11; @VN-24].
2. **Currency devaluation.** Price in another 2.5 to 3% fall [@VN-58].
3. **Profits leave once a year.** After audited accounts and tax finalisation [@VN-7].
4. **Bank buffers are thin.** 5.3% bad loans, lending above deposits [@VN-8].
5. **Power is the bottleneck.** A 4,200 MW shortfall expected in 2027 [@VN-55].

</Fragment>

</Slide>

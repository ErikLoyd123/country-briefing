<Slide title="What that does to your costs" lede="Budget for rising costs in both, but the levers are different." sgMedia="singapore/orchard-street.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/motorbikes-at-lights.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Prices:** MAS expects 1.5 to 2.5% inflation this year [@SG-15] and is letting the dollar rise faster [@SG-3].
- **Currency:** a firmer dollar makes local payroll and leases dearer in US-dollar terms. Consider hedging them.
- **Hiring:** layoffs in manufacturing, infocomm and finance free up experienced people for regional roles [@SG-17].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **Prices:** inflation has averaged 4.45% so far this year [@VN-16]. Put cost-escalation clauses in supplier and lease contracts.
- **Pay:** average income is about US$340 a month [@VN-18], still a clear cost advantage.
- **Skills:** with under a third of workers formally qualified [@VN-18], skilled talent costs more than averages suggest.
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="The public finances" lede={"Singapore runs surpluses and owns more than it owes.\nVietnam borrows moderately to build."}>

<Fragment slot="sg">
<BigStat value="1.0%" unit="of GDP budget surplus, FY2026">
S$8.5bn, after 1.9% in FY2025 [@SG-18].
</BigStat>

- **Debt looks high, isn't:** about 99% is raised for non-spending purposes, and the government has more assets than debts [@SG-20].
- **Watch:** the Pillar Two top-up tax lifts large multinationals to a 15% effective rate from FY2027 [@SG-19]. Model it now.
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
<BigStat value="~36%" unit="of GDP public debt">
With a 2025 deficit near 3.6% of GDP [@VN-19].
</BigStat>

- **Room to build:** moderate debt leaves space for projects such as a US$67bn high-speed railway from Hanoi [@VN-20].
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="The long run" lede={"Singapore is settling into maturity.\nVietnam is aiming high, and forecasters expect less than the targets."}>

<Fragment slot="sg">
<BigStat value="2 to 3%" unit="a year, next decade">
The government's own long-run growth estimate [@SG-22].
</BigStat>

- Modest by regional standards. Plan around it.
- Use Singapore for control, stability and access to the region; look to faster neighbours for volume.
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
<BigStat value="10.2%" unit="Ho Chi Minh City target, 2026">
With output per person of US$9,800 [@VN-24]. That needs nearly 12% growth in the second half [@VN-11].
</BigStat>

- **Milestone:** upper-middle income as of July 2026 [@VN-25], aiming for high income by 2045 [@VN-20].
- **Reality check:** AMRO expects 7.4% national growth for 2026 [@VN-20].
</Fragment>

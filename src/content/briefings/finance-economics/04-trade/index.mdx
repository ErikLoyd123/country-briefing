<Slide title="How much they trade" lede="Both are trading at record pace in 2026. Singapore's exports are surging; Vietnam's imports are outrunning its exports.">

<Fragment slot="sg">
<BigStat value="S$1.4tn" unit="goods trade, 2025">
Up 8.7% on 2024 [@SG-23].
</BigStat>

- **2026 is faster still:** exports up 49.2% and imports 39.3% in August alone, year on year [@SG-24].
- **What's driving it:** electronics exports up 131.8%, on AI-related demand [@SG-24].
- **The mix:** machinery and transport equipment make up about 69% of non-oil exports [@SG-25].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
<BigStat value="US$930bn" unit="goods trade, 2025">
A record, with a US$20bn surplus [@VN-26].
</BigStat>

- **2026 has flipped:** exports up 22.4% but imports up 35.3% through August [@VN-27].
- **The result:** a US$23.5bn deficit by mid-September, the largest in 30 years [@VN-28].
- **Where it sits:** domestic firms run the deficit; foreign-invested firms still run a surplus [@VN-28].
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="Trade agreements" lede="Both are in the region's big deals. Singapore's network is wider; Vietnam's can't replace the US market.">

<Fragment slot="sg">
<BigStat value="29" unit="free trade agreements in force">
Including CPTPP, RCEP and the EU–Singapore FTA [@SG-29].
</BigStat>

- **The hedge is the network,** not any single deal: it spreads exposure across many markets.
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
<BigStat value="18" unit="FTAs, in force and planned">
Including CPTPP (2019), RCEP (2022) and the EU–Vietnam FTA (2020) [@VN-33].
</BigStat>

- **RCEP alone** covers about 30% of the world's people and output [@VN-33].
- **A buffer, not a replacement:** no agreement matches the scale of the US market.
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="The US tariff risk" tone="tint" lede="Both now face the same new 12.5% US duty. Vietnam has far more at stake.">

<Fragment slot="sg">
<BigStat value="12.5%" unit="extra US duty from 24 July 2026">
Part of a forced-labour action covering 60 economies [@SG-30].
</BigStat>

- **Exposed:** about a third of domestic exports to the US, S$9.5bn a year, roughly 3% of domestic exports globally [@SG-31].
- **So far:** MTI calls the impact muted [@SG-31].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
<BigStat value="12.5%" unit="extra US duty from 24 July 2026">
The same flat Section 301 rate [@VN-36].
</BigStat>

- **Before that:** a 20% reciprocal tariff from August 2025, cut to 10% in February 2026 after a Supreme Court ruling [@VN-35].
- **Still contested:** 25 US states are suing over the new duty [@VN-37].
- **At stake:** US$137bn of two-way trade through August, up 23.4% [@VN-32].
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="Where supply chains could break" lede="Singapore's risks are chokepoints and inputs. Vietnam's growth rests on firms that moved from China." sgMedia="singapore/pasir-panjang-terminal.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/binh-duong-industrial-park.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
- **Sea lanes:** Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia pledged in August to keep the Malacca and Singapore Straits open [@SG-32].
- **Helium:** under half of Singapore's chip fabs recycle it, vs 70%+ in Japan and Taiwan [@SG-33].
- **Resilience:** 23% of firms could run four to six months through a shock, vs 13% globally [@SG-34].
- **Planning ahead:** a first-of-its-kind essential-supplies pact with New Zealand took effect in July [@SG-35].
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
- **China+1:** Chinese-owned firms account for a disproportionate share of the export boom to the US [@VN-38].
- **Still tied to China:** those firms keep sourcing their inputs there [@VN-38].
- **Moving up:** a national chip strategy offers up to 50% subsidies for semiconductor R&D centres [@VN-39].
- **Handle with care:** the shift may owe as much to trade tensions as to lasting advantage.
</Fragment>

</Slide>

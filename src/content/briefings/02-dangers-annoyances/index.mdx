<Slide title="Government" lede="In Singapore the risk is what you say. In Vietnam it is who you deal with.">

<Fragment slot="sg">
<RiskCard country="SG" area="government" level="low" title="Stable to the point of predictability, with tight rules on speech">
The PAP won 87 of 97 seats in 2025 [@SG-17]. Foreigners need a police permit even to join an event at Speakers' Corner [@SG-27].

<Fragment slot="means">keep corporate voice on commercial matters.</Fragment>
</RiskCard>
<RiskCard country="SG" area="government" level="low" title="Religious and racial harmony is law, not etiquette">
Speech that wounds religious feelings is an offence [@SG-29].

<Fragment slot="means">marketing and staff posts get legal review, not just brand review.</Fragment>
</RiskCard>
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
<RiskCard country="VN" area="government" level="high" title="One party, a lot of change, and a purge that reaches the top">
A president resigned in 2024 [@VN-24]. Provinces were cut from 63 to 34 and ministries from 30 to 22 [@VN-21; @VN-22].

<Fragment slot="means">build relationships with the institution, not one official, and expect delay [@VN-66].</Fragment>
</RiskCard>
<RiskCard country="VN" area="government" level="high" title="Data lives here now">
Foreign digital firms can be ordered to store user data locally [@VN-25]; data-transfer fines reach 5 percent of revenue [@VN-26].

<Fragment slot="means">anything with a login or a customer database needs a Vietnam data plan.</Fragment>
</RiskCard>
<RiskCard country="VN" area="government" level="low" title="The reform is real too">
The 2026 investment law cut 38 conditional business lines [@VN-29].

<Fragment slot="means">entry is easier on paper; test it on the November visits.</Fragment>
</RiskCard>
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="Society" lede="Singapore is old and expensive. Vietnam is young, but short of skills and clean air." sgMedia="singapore/public-housing.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/cho-ray-hospital.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
<RiskCard country="SG" area="society" level="mid" title="Old, expensive, and getting more so">
One citizen in five is 65 or older, heading to one in four by 2030 [@SG-34]; often named the world's most expensive city [@SG-35].

<Fragment slot="means">expatriate packages cost more and the home market is small.</Fragment>
</RiskCard>
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
<RiskCard country="VN" area="society" level="mid" title="Young, willing, and under-credentialed">
52.9 million workers, but only 28.8 percent hold a diploma or certificate [@VN-33].

<Fragment slot="means">labour is available; skilled supervisors are not. Budget for training.</Fragment>
</RiskCard>
<RiskCard country="VN" area="society" level="mid" title="You will breathe the air">
Hanoi was the 10th most polluted major city in January 2026 [@VN-30]; local hospitals do not take US insurance [@VN-9].

<Fragment slot="means">insure senior staff for evacuation.</Fragment>
</RiskCard>
</Fragment>

</Slide>

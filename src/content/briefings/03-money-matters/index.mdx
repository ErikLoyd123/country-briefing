<Slide title="Open doors and closed ones" covers={['FDI']} sgMedia="singapore/supreme-court-night.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/saigon-skyline.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">
<div class="[&_li]:min-h-[2lh]">

- **Open:** most sectors, with no blanket cap on foreign ownership [@SG-56].
- **Partly open:** foreign holdings in broadcasting must stay under 49% [@SG-56].
- **Closed:** landed homes, unless the government approves the sale [@SG-57].
- **Tightening:** since March 2024, a stake of 12% or more in a firm critical to national security needs ministerial approval [@SG-104].

</div>
</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">
<div class="[&_li]:min-h-[2lh]">

- **Open:** 100% foreign ownership in manufacturing-adjacent services, consultancy, hotels and IT [@VN-57].
- **Partly open:** 58 restricted business lines, with 49% caps in areas such as waterway transport [@VN-57].
- **Closed:** 25 business lines, and land itself. You hold use rights by allocation, lease or transfer [@VN-57; @VN-61].
- **Tightening:** industrial parks lost their tax incentives for projects licensed after October 2025 [@VN-58].

</div>
</Fragment>

</Slide>

<Slide title="What it means for an investor" covers={['FDI']} layout="joined">

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
<Takeaway media="singapore/raffles-place.jpg" label="Singapore">
The tax break is shorter, but your money moves whenever you like.

<Fragment slot="detail">No capital controls and no dividend withholding [@SG-60; @SG-61]; incentives run five years at most [@SG-58].</Fragment>
</Takeaway>
<Takeaway media="vietnam/binh-duong-industrial-park.jpg" label="Vietnam">
The tax break is longer, but your cash leaves once a year, and only if the books show a profit.

<Fragment slot="detail">Remittance waits for audited accounts and tax finalisation, and accumulated losses block it [@VN-60]. Large groups still pay at least 15% [@VN-59].</Fragment>
</Takeaway>
</div>

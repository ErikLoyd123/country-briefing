<Slide title="Open doors and closed ones" covers={['FDI']} sgMedia="singapore/supreme-court-night.jpg" vnMedia="vietnam/saigon-skyline.jpg">

<Fragment slot="sg">

- **Open:** no blanket cap on foreign ownership [@SG-56]; a company costs S$315 in government fees to set up [@SG-64].
- **Growing:** S$14.2 billion of new fixed-asset commitments in 2025 [@SG-65].
- **Closed:** broadcasting stakes of 49 percent or more [@SG-56], and landed homes without approval [@SG-57].

</Fragment>

<Fragment slot="vn">

- **Open:** 100 percent foreign ownership in manufacturing-adjacent services, consultancy, hotels and IT [@VN-57].
- **Partly open:** 49 percent caps in areas such as waterway transport [@VN-57].
- **Shrinking:** industrial parks lost their tax incentives for projects licensed after October 2025 [@VN-58].
- **Closed:** land ownership. You hold use rights by allocation, lease or transfer [@VN-61].

</Fragment>

</Slide>

The tax break is shorter, but your money moves whenever you like.

<Fragment slot="detail">No capital controls and no dividend withholding [@SG-60; @SG-61]; incentives run five years at most [@SG-58].</Fragment>
</Takeaway>
<Takeaway media="vietnam/binh-duong-industrial-park.jpg" label="Vietnam">
The tax break is longer, but your cash leaves once a year, and only if the books show a profit.

<Fragment slot="detail">Remittance waits for audited accounts and tax finalisation, and accumulated losses block it [@VN-60]. Large groups still pay at least 15 percent [@VN-59].</Fragment>
</Takeaway>
